The package stage preserves Electron `LICENSE.electron.txt` separately from Pliego’s root `LICENSE`; Chromium’s `LICENSES.chromium.html` also remains in the packaged Electron runtime. The PDF.js build copies all of `standard_fonts/` and `wasm/`, including their bundled `LICENSE_*` files, and `third-party-licenses/pdfjs-dist/LICENSE` is included in the notice tree. This inventory is not a legal compatibility certification.
The separately published `isarray@1.0.0` package omits its LICENSE file; its text is reproduced from the upstream project’s LICENSE (https://github.com/juliangruber/isarray/blob/master/LICENSE). The `marked-footnote@1.4.0` package tarball omits the license text; its upstream package metadata declares MIT and its README identifies Stilearning © 2023-2024 (https://github.com/bent10/marked-extensions/tree/main/packages/footnote). The standard MIT terms and that copyright attribution are preserved in its license file.

For packages offering license choices, Pliego selects JSZip’s MIT option and DOMPurify’s Apache-2.0 option; the complete source package dual-license texts are retained. Pako’s MIT text and the zlib license text are both retained. PDF.js is Apache-2.0; the packaged standard-font and wasm directories carry their upstream asset-specific license files.
